The gap here is almost entirely brokerage: STT, exchange charges, SEBI charges, and stamp duty are government/exchange-set rates every broker pays identically. The discount brokers (Dhan, Zerodha, Fyers, INDmoney, Groww, Angel One, Upstox) charge ₹0–40 in brokerage; the full-service brokers (Kotak, ICICI Direct, HDFC Securities) charge a percentage of turnover instead, which scales up fast on a larger trade.

The honest takeaway

There's no single "cheapest broker" — it depends on your trade size, how often you trade delivery vs. intraday, and whether you value a discount broker's low cost over a full-service broker's research/advisory desk. What matters is knowing the real number for your actual trades, not a rate card's headline percentage.

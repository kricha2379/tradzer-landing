Broker Charges Guide

Zerodha Brokerage Calculator: Delivery, Intraday, STT & All Charges Explained

Updated for 2026 · Rates sourced directly from zerodha.com/charges/

Zerodha's own charges page lists every rate, but doesn't actually add them up for you. Here's exactly what gets deducted from a Zerodha equity trade — delivery and intraday — with a real worked example at the end.

Delivery trades

  • Brokerage: ₹0. Zerodha charges no brokerage at all on equity delivery.
  • STT (Securities Transaction Tax): 0.1% of turnover, charged on both the buy and the sell.
  • Exchange transaction charge: 0.00307% of turnover (NSE), both legs.
  • SEBI charges: 0.0001% of turnover, both legs — tiny, but real.
  • Stamp duty: 0.015% of turnover, buy leg only (a state government levy, standard across every broker, not Zerodha-specific).
  • DP (Depository Participant) charge: ₹13.00 flat per scrip when you sell (₹12.75 for a female demat holder) — a CDSL pass-through fee (₹3.50 / ₹3.25) plus Zerodha's own ₹9.50 flat fee, for moving shares out of your demat account. Not a brokerage fee.
  • GST: 18%, applied on brokerage + exchange charges + SEBI charges + the DP charge (not on STT or stamp duty).

Intraday trades

  • Brokerage: 0.03% of turnover or ₹20 per executed order, whichever is lower — per leg.
  • STT: 0.025% of turnover, sell leg only (intraday STT is much lower than delivery's 0.1%).
  • Exchange transaction charge: same 0.00307%, both legs.
  • Stamp duty: 0.003% of turnover, buy leg only.
  • DP charge: none — the shares never actually enter your demat account on an intraday trade, so this charge doesn't apply.

Worked example: Delivery trade

Buy 100 shares at ₹1,000, sell at ₹1,050 (a ₹5,000 gross profit before charges):

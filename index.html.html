WHY A LEDGER, NOT JUST A LOG

Logging trades is easy. Learning from them isn't.

Most journals stop at profit and loss. A ledger is supposed to tell you something truer than that — where the money actually came from, and where your own habits helped or hurt. "Tradzer" is Trade + Ledger -- that's the whole idea in one word.

01

Discipline, measured

Every trade gets a short "Judgment Call" checklist. Over time, Tradzer splits your closed trades by how much of your own checklist you actually followed — and shows you, honestly, whether that discipline is paying off.

02

Real broker math

Brokerage, STT, stamp duty, exchange charges, GST, DP charges — calculated from each broker's own published rates, not a rough guess. Eight major Indian brokers built in, plus a custom option for any other.

03

One home, every account

Track trades across multiple brokers and currencies in one place, without losing the ability to see each one separately when it matters.
